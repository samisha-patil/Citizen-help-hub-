🌆 About Pune

Complete information about Pune City

🏙️ Pune City Introduction

Pune is one of the major cities of Maharashtra, India. It is known for its history, education, culture, industries, information technology and pleasant surroundings.

Pune is located in western Maharashtra and is situated near the Mula-Mutha river system. The city has developed into an important educational, industrial and IT centre.

Pune is also popularly associated with the title "Oxford of the East" because of its large number of educational institutions.

📜 History of Pune

Pune has a long and important history in Maharashtra. The region has been associated with several historical dynasties and rulers.

During the Maratha period, Pune became an important political centre. The Peshwas played a major role in the development of the city.

Many historical places such as Shaniwar Wada and Lal Mahal reflect Pune's historical importance.

🗺️ Geography & Location

Pune is located in western Maharashtra. The city lies in the Deccan Plateau region and is surrounded by hills and several natural areas.

The Mula and Mutha rivers are important geographical features associated with Pune.

🌦️ Climate

Pune generally experiences a tropical wet and dry climate. The city has summer, monsoon and winter seasons.

☀️ Summer

Summer months are generally warm, especially during the daytime.

🌧️ Monsoon

Pune receives rainfall mainly during the monsoon season.

❄️ Winter

Winter is generally cooler and more comfortable compared with summer.

🎓 Education

Pune is one of India's important educational centres. Students from different parts of Maharashtra and India come to Pune for higher education.

  • Universities and colleges
  • Engineering and technology institutes
  • Medical and management institutions
  • Research and educational centres
  • Vocational and skill-development institutes

🎭 Culture & Festivals

🎉 Ganesh Festival
